## Overview

I am a scientist and technical co-founder with over a decade of experience spanning research and engineering across academia and industry. I build trustworthy, adaptive AI systems engineered to remain correct under real-world pressure: verifiable, robust, and continually learning.

After a DPhil at the [University of Oxford](https://www.ox.ac.uk) on computational methods for DNA sequencing data, I moved into quantum computing at [Quantinuum](https://www.quantinuum.com) and [Pasqal](https://www.pasqal.com), developing quantum scientific machine learning software and algorithms. As Staff Engineer in AI at [Thermo Fisher Scientific](https://www.thermofisher.com), I worked on deep learning models for cryo-electron microscopy images. I am now a co-founder and lead researcher at [Sophic Labs](https://sophiclabs.eu).

## Portfolio

- **[Sophic Labs](https://sophiclabs.eu)**: Builds AI from first principles for the pressures of production: continual adaptation, verifiable behaviour and mathematical correctness.

## Research

- **Verifiable and adaptive AI**: Systems with formal behavioural bounds, verifiable guarantees and stable continual adaptation.
- **Algorithm and method development**: Computational and machine learning algorithms that combine rigour with scalable, resource-efficient execution.
- **Machine learning for the physical and biological sciences**: Applied machine learning and computational methods for domains where data are limited, noisy, or constrained by underlying mechanics.
